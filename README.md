# Zomato-Restaurant-Data-Analysis-Using-Python
# 🍽️ Restaurant & Review Analysis using Zomato Dataset

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes restaurant and review data from the Zomato dataset to discover valuable insights into customer preferences, restaurant ratings, popular cuisines, pricing trends, and location-based restaurant distribution. The analysis helps identify the key factors affecting restaurant ratings and provides business recommendations for an Alfido Tech-style food discovery platform.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and preprocess the restaurant dataset.
- Handle missing values and inconsistent data.
- Analyze restaurant ratings and customer preferences.
- Explore cuisine popularity and restaurant hotspots.
- Study the relationship between price and ratings.
- Create informative visualizations.
- Provide business recommendations based on the analysis.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Most restaurant ratings range between **3.5 and 4.2**.
- North Indian, Chinese, and South Indian cuisines are the most popular.
- Restaurant hotspots are concentrated in commercial areas.
- Restaurants offering online ordering generally receive higher customer engagement.
- Customer votes show a positive relationship with restaurant ratings.
